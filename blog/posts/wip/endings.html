what makes me who i am, which little parts of me can i leave behind? is it the small carillion they gave me when i was born, my MOBILI i've slowly filled over the years, my first copy of harry potter, crumpled up and dyed by the tea i spilled on it while on vacation to the mountains? is it the beer caps i take home to put in a small box, the clothes i got for 1euro at the thrift shop that "i'll find a way to style"? is it my little sister's side of the room, her lights hanging on the wall and my old childhood books now in her bookshelves?
[quale piccolo dettaglio fa la differenza tra me e qualcun altro - e se mi dimentico proprio quell'oggetto che mi permette di dire "ah, ecco chi sono - sono aglaia"? se per sbaglio porto con me qualcun altro? qualcuno che non sono io, qualcuno che non posso essere? ] [quale piccolo dettaglio fa la differenza tra me e una persona incompleta? what small detail makes the difference between me and an incomplete person?]
and what small detail makes the difference between me and someone else - and what if i forget that exact thing that allows me to say "ah, that's who i am - that's aglaia"? what if, by mistake, i bring someone else with me? someone i am not, someone i cannot be? how do i find that one small trinket that perfectly completes the puzzle, and do i even want to bring myself? and can i trust those parts of myself to boxes left in old garages, or will the dust wash the magic away?

and how much of ourselves is in our homes? my room has always been dear to me, with the walls i spent years filling with the perfect collage of pictures and my new CD collection and the colors !! and am i really myself if i don't have that yellow wall in the kitchen and those dim lights?
